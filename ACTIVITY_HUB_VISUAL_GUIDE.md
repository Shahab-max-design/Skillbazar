## 🎯 Key Design Principles

✅ **No Discovery Elements** - Search, categories, professional browsing removed
✅ **Activity Focused** - Shows customer's own bookings, messages, payments
✅ **Management Hub** - Full control over account, profile, bookings
✅ **Emerald Theme** - Consistent green accent throughout
✅ **Executive Summary** - Dashboard shows key insights at a glance
✅ **Functional Data** - All cards show real/mock functional data
✅ **Responsive** - Mobile, tablet, desktop optimized
✅ **Professional** - Clean, modern, business-like appearance
